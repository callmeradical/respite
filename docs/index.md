    details: Month-view calendar that fills your window. Color-coded pills for scheduled, taken, and holiday days. Blue circle for today, Apple Calendar style.

  - icon: 📈
    title: Accrual engine
    details: Bi-weekly or semi-monthly pay periods. Configurable year-end carryover limit in hours. Opening balance, accrual cap, and hours-per-day conversion all in one settings panel.

  - icon: ✨
    title: Time-off optimizer
    details: Finds windows where a few PTO days yield the most consecutive days off. Accounts for both accrued and yet-to-earn days. Ranks by total days × efficiency so longer breaks win.

  - icon: 🗓️
    title: Holiday management
    details: Import all 11 US federal holidays for any year with one click, or paste a custom list in any format. Observance shifts (Sat→Fri, Sun→Mon) computed automatically.

  - icon: 📤
    title: ICS export
    details: Export selected PTO entries as a standards-compliant .ics file. Pick exactly which events to include. Opens directly in Calendar, Outlook, or Google Calendar.

  - icon: 🔒
    title: Local-first
    details: All data lives in a SQLite database on your Mac. No cloud sync, no accounts, no subscriptions. Share the DMG with a colleague and they start fresh.
