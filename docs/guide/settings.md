# Settings

Open Settings from the **⚙ button** at the bottom of the left drawer.

## Appearance

| Option | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Light | Always light mode |
| Auto | Follows the macOS system preference |
| Dark | Always dark mode |

## Pay period cadence

| Option | Pay periods / year |
|---|---|
| Bi-weekly | 26 (every 14 days) |
| Semi-monthly | 24 (1st and 15th of each month) |

## Accrual

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| PTO days per year | Total days you earn annually (e.g. 15, 20) |
| Accrual start date | When tracking began — usually your hire date or Jan 1 |
| Opening balance | Days already held on the start date |
| Hours per working day | Conversion factor for hours ↔ days display (default 8) |

## Year-end carryover

| Option | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Unlimited | Full balance rolls into the new year |
| Cap carryover | Enter a maximum in **hours** (e.g. 40 hrs = 5 days at 8 hrs/day) |

At year-end, any balance above the cap is forfeited. The balance tile shows a green ✓ badge when you're within the limit and a red ▲ badge when you're over.

## Accrual cap (continuous)

An optional hard ceiling on your running balance. Accrual pauses once you reach this level. Distinct from the year-end carryover limit — this one applies continuously throughout the year.
